About

I started the way most front-end people do — writing HTML, CSS and JavaScript by hand, line by line, at StartNg. That habit of understanding what's under the hood never left, even after I moved into visual, no-code-first tools.

Today I design and ship production websites almost entirely in Webflow and Framer: marketing sites, product landing pages, and CMS-driven content sites for startups and studios who need something that looks considered and ships in weeks, not quarters. I care about the same things a developer cares about — semantics, performance, accessibility — but I get there through a canvas instead of a terminal.
